Ledger privacy policy

Operated by xRiveria. Effective 11 October 2026. This policy describes the current release candidate and will be updated if its data practices change.

Financial records stay on your device

Ledger has no registration, finance backend, advertising SDK or analytics SDK. Expenses, income, subscriptions, manually entered balances, money owed, budgets, salary plans, categories and profile names are stored locally. The two profiles are separate workspaces, not password-protected accounts. Ledger does not send these finance records to an application server.

Exports and application updates

Export Data creates CSV or JSON locally. If you choose Share, the operating system passes the export to your selected destination. That destination’s retention and privacy practices apply to its copy. Ledger does not automatically upload exports.

Expo provides app-code updates and may process technical information such as app/runtime version, platform, installation identifiers and network information to deliver compatible updates. Finance records are not included in update requests. See Expo’s privacy policy for its service practices. The operating system may include local app records in device backups according to your settings.

Deletion and retention

Edit or delete records in Ledger. Settings clears entries or resets the active profile, including its salary plan, without resetting the other profile. Appearance is app-wide. There is no remote Ledger account to delete. Removing the app can remove local finance storage. Exports and backups remain under your control and their destinations’ retention rules. Legacy appearance preferences in the iOS keychain may survive uninstall.

Support and this website

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical information about visits under its privacy policy. If you submit a support report, your GitHub username and the information you provide are public and retained with that issue. Avoid submitting sensitive information. Contact xRiveria through the support form for questions about this policy or a request concerning a support report.

Contact support

Use the GitHub support form to report a problem or ask a question. A GitHub account is required. Include the app version, iOS version and steps to reproduce. Reports are public: do not attach financial records, bank details, home/work addresses, API keys or other private information.
